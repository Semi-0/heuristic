## language-cell
(define name expression) binds a cell. (network [parameters] body) creates a callable. A network body can contain local define forms and return its final expression, including a struct. (struct :key value ...) constructs connected fields. Read a field with ((slot :key) object). (select condition then-value else-value) selects a branch. round-money rounds to two decimals, half up; use it only on the final amount. str builds text from connected values. Unknown inputs leave dependent computations unresolved. ttms-premise-closure attaches a premise to a callable; invocation carries it to results. ## rules-cell
Fictional print-shop rules: progressive production pricing: first tier-one-limit copies at tier-one-price each; next tier-two-count copies at tier-two-price each; remaining copies at tier-three-price each. Rush service adds rush-rate times production cost. Members receive member-rate times production plus rush charges as discount, capped by the order discount-cap; nonmembers receive no discount. Add setup-fee after discount. Delivery costs delivery-fee, waived when subtotal after discount and setup is at least free-delivery-threshold. Apply tax-rate to the entire amount including delivery. Final total includes tax and is rounded half up to cents; intermediate values are not rounded.

## metadata-cell
pricing exposes the shared numeric rules. Every order-N contains independently supported :quantity, :rush? boolean, :member? boolean, :discount-cap, and :question premise. question premises identify the corresponding requested quote. shared-rule identifies the validity premise for the general pricing rules. Keep independent orders separate. A promotional discount-cap belongs only to the order carrying it. delivery-note is unrelated. Quote output slots are production cost, rush charge, discount amount, subtotal after setup, delivery charge, tax amount, and final rounded total. Future orders are unknown until asked.
