Queued at 01:56 UTC on November 27, after the four-way prompt experiment. Six matched historical and revised orders use the recorded definitions and quantities from the November 26 reuse experiment. Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B scores the label and body of every archived record against the exact speaker question, with one fixed instruction. Top-one selection is fixed before evaluation. The harness expands the selected record's transitive Datomic parents and sends the ordered evidence to the existing Flash xhigh solver. Further reads are available and counted. The expected answer and gold dependency IDs are available only to the scorer.

Timing records per-question ranking, end-to-end request time and cold model loading separately. Context assembly and compiler scoring are included in total time. The solver and compiler settings match the [original reuse trial](2026-11-26-reuse-context.md). Earlier conditions are historical comparisons.
